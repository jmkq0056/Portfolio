Startup · gathrsolutions.dk

        
Gathr Solutions

        

          

            
The problem

            
Danish security firms run on a pile of separate tools. Spreadsheets for shifts, paper for patrol logs, phone calls when something goes wrong.

          

          

            
Planning

            
Gathr is built to replace the pile. Drag and drop shift planning on top of an employee database that knows certifications and availability.

          

          

            
On the ground

            
Patrols are verified at NFC, QR, BLE and GPS checkpoints, with incident alerts the moment something happens. On top of that: time tracking, reporting and a customer portal.

          

          

            
Operations

            
I designed it, built it and run it: data model, APIs, web, mobile with offline support, auth, CI/CD, hosting. Live on the App Store and Google Play. An early stage startup with real interest from Danish security companies.
